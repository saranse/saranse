## 🛡️ About

I work in **defensive security**, helping teams improve visibility, detect suspicious activity, and respond to incidents with useful context. I enjoy connecting the dots between endpoint, network, and SIEM data—and making repeatable security work easier through automation.

My goal: **less noise, better detections, and faster time from alert to understanding.**

| Focus | What that means |

## ⚙️ How I approach the work

- **Start with the signal:** understand what the data can and cannot tell us.
- **Add context:** connect events, assets, identities, and timelines before deciding what happened.
- **Make detections useful:** aim for clear logic, actionable alerts, and fewer false positives.
- **Automate carefully:** make common tasks repeatable, auditable, and easier to maintain.
- **Keep learning:** revisit assumptions as systems, threats, and telemetry change.
